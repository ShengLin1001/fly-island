## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.4 的第一个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **歌词上岛（新）**：设置 → 音乐 → 歌词 里打开「在收起的岛上显示当前歌词」，播放时收起的岛显示正在唱的那一句；暂停、间奏和没找到歌词时照常显示待办。同一处可以选 AI Agent 运行时谁优先：默认歌词优先，Agent 会话开始、完成或出错时临时显示 4 秒，等你确认或回答的会话一直显示到你处理。
- **音乐页重做**：和 Agent 页一样直接画在岛上，没有单独的卡片，浅色岛屿上文字自动变深。正在唱的那句太长时折成两行；能读到播放进度的播放器显示进度条和时间。网易云这类不报进度的播放器，岛屿从换歌时开始计时，点任意一句歌词就能把时间对齐到那一句。
- **网易云精确同步**：已装 BetterNCM 时，可以在 设置 → 音乐 → 歌词 里一键安装 InfLink-rs，按歌曲直接查歌词，更准。中文界面默认同时查酷狗和网易云的歌词。
- **待办的「岛屿」样式（新）**：设置 → 待办 → 样式 可选「纸张」（默认，原来的横线笔记本）或「岛屿」（和岛上其他页面一样的列表，用文字标签切换今天、笔记、即将到来和归档）。
- **弹出提醒更紧凑**：新消息和 Agent 提醒自动弹出时不再显示岛的顶部栏，岛缩窄到只包住卡片；点「全部消息」或卡片外的空白处展开成完整页面。审批 / 提问卡片仍是全宽。
- **悬停切换页面（新）**：设置 → 通用 → 展开与收起 里打开「悬停切换页面」，鼠标停在展开岛顶部的页面圆点上就切过去，停留时间可调（0–1 秒）。
- **邮箱图标**：每个邮箱显示自己服务商的图标（QQ 邮箱、网易、Gmail、Outlook），其他邮箱用通用的邮件图标。
- **修复**：
  - 经典外观的收起岛不再有一条灰色渐变，点了岛上的按钮后边缘也不会一直亮着。
  - 岛展开、收起时 Agent 行和文字不再上下抖动一个像素。

插件市场同时更新了（在插件市场里更新即可）：「正在播放」1.2.0 改成歌名和歌手，旁边是音乐软件的图标，弹出面板里只有它一条时整个岛就是这条提示；系统监控 1.0.3 可以在后台记录图表，图表跨度改成 5 分钟。插件作者：`island.notify` 新增 `icon` 和 `layout`（能力 `notifyStyle`），见插件文档。

## About this release

This is the first beta of 0.3.4. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **Lyrics on the island (new)**: turn on "Show the current line on the collapsed island" under Settings → Music → Lyrics, and while a song plays the collapsed island shows the line being sung; paused, between verses or without lyrics it shows your todo as usual. The same place chooses what comes first while an AI agent runs: lyrics by default, with an agent showing for 4 seconds when a session starts, finishes or fails, and a session waiting for your approval or answer staying until you deal with it.
- **Music page redone**: it sits straight on the island like the Agent page, with no card of its own, and its text turns dark on a light island. A long line wraps to two lines; players that report their position get a progress bar and times. For players that do not (such as NetEase), the island times the song from the change, and clicking any lyric line aligns the timing to it.
- **Exact NetEase sync**: with BetterNCM installed, Settings → Music → Lyrics installs InfLink-rs in one click, which looks lyrics up by the song itself. On a Chinese system, KuGou and NetEase lyrics are on by default.
- **Island style for todos (new)**: Settings → Todos → Style offers Paper (default, the lined notebook) or Island (a list like the island's other pages, with named tabs for Today, Note, Upcoming and Archive).
- **Tighter popups**: new messages and Agent alerts that open by themselves no longer show the island's header, and the island narrows to the card; "All messages" or a click beside the card opens the whole page. Approval and question cards keep the full width.
- **Switch pages on hover (new)**: turn it on under Settings → General → Opening and resting the pointer on a page dot of the expanded island opens that page; the hover time is adjustable (0–1 s).
- **Mailbox logos**: each mailbox shows its service's logo (QQ Mail, NetEase, Gmail, Outlook); others show the plain mail icon.
- **Fixes**:
  - The collapsed island in the classic look no longer shows a grey gradient, and its edges no longer stay lit after clicking one of its buttons.
  - Agent rows and text no longer step a pixel while the island opens and closes.

Plugins in the market were updated too (update them there): Now playing 1.2.0 shows the song and artist beside the player's icon, and alone in the popup it is the whole island; System monitor 1.0.3 can record its charts in the background, which now span five minutes. For plugin authors: `island.notify` takes `icon` and `layout` (capability `notifyStyle`); see the plugin docs.
